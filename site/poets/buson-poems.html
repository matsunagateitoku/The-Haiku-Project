Summer
病人の駕も過けり麥の秋
byonin no / kago mo sugi keri / mugi no aki
a sick man's / palanquin also pass by / wheat's autumn
かはぼりやむかひの女房こちを見る
Kawahori ya / mukai no nyobo / kochi wo miru
Bats — / the wife across the street / looks this way.
月の句を吐いてへらさん蟾の腹
Tsuki no ku wo / haite herasan / hiki no hara
If he’d spit out / a ku on the moon, the toad’s belly / might shrink.
夏川を越すうれしさよ手に草履
Natsu gawa wo / kosu ureshisa yo / te ni zori
How joyful to cross / a summer river! / Sandals in hand.
山々を低く覚ゆる青田かな
Yama yama wo / hikuku oboyuru / aota kana
All the mountains / seem lower, by / green ricefields.
更衣野路の人はつかに白し
Koromogae / noji no hito wazu / kani shireshi
Change of garments — / on field paths people / look whiter.
羅に遮る蓮のにほひかな
Usumono ni / saegiru hasu no / nioi kana
By a thin silk / kimono screened, the / scent of lotus.
細脛に夕風さはるたかむしろ
Hoso hagi ni / yu kaze sawaru / takamushiro
On the thin legs / a dusk breeze — / bamboo mat.
後家の君たそがれ顔のうちはかな
Goke no kimi / tasogare gao no / uchiwa kana
The widow — / her face at dusk, behind / a round fan.
風鈴や花にはつらき風ながら
Furin ya / hana seiun tsuraki / kaze nagare
Windbells — / though for flowers the / wind was cruel.
朝風に　毛を吹かれ居る　毛虫かな
Asa kaze ni / ke wo fukare iru / kemushi kana
In the morning breeze / its fur being blown — / caterpillar.
わくらばに　取り付いて　蝉の　もぬけかな
Wakuraba ni / tori tsuite semi no / monuke kana
Clinging to a / worm-eaten leaf, a cicada's / discarded shell.
蠅散って　且う　白しや　盆の糊
Hae chitte / katsu shiroshi ya / bon no nori
The flies scatter, / for a moment white — / the tray's starch.
点滴に　うたれて籠る　蝸牛
Tenteki ni / utarete komoru / katatsumuri
Hit by a drop / of water, the snail withdraws / from the world.
牡丹散りて　うちかさなりぬ　二三片
Botan chirite / uchi kasanarinu / ni san ben
A peony scatters — / one on another, two, / three petals.
青梅に　眉　あつめたる　美人哉
Ao ume ni / mayei atsume taru / bijin kana
At the green plum / her eyebrows gathered, / a beautiful woman.
不二ひとつ　うづみ残して　わかばかな
Fuji hitotsu / uzumi nokoshite / wakaba kana
Only Mt. Fuji / left uncovered — / young leaves.
三井寺や　日は午にせまる　若楓
Miidera ya / hi wa go ni semaru / waka kaede
Mii temple — / the sun presses on to noon, / young maple leaves.
杜若　べたりと　鳶のたれてける
Kakitsubate / betari to tobi no / tarete keru
Flower de luce — / "plop", a kite dropped / his dung.
