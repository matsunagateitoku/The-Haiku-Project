植田  ·  4 poems
胴亀や昨日植ゑたる田の濁り
Dogame ya / kino uetaru / ta no nigori
A salt-marsh turtle — the muddiness of a paddy planted yesterday.
Kyoroku
我かものに植田の蛙鳴つのる
Waga mono ni / ueta no kawazu / naki tsunoru
As if their own planted ricefields, frogs' loud songs.
Gyodai
植ゑつけし夜は三日月の門田かな
Uetsukeshi / yo wa mikazuki no / kakota kana
The night they were planted, a crescent moon — gate ricefields.
Seira
大雨に濁りかへせし植田哉
Oame ni / nigori kaeseshi / ueta kana
With a downpour their muddiness returned, planted ricefields.
