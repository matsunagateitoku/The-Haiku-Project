FIXES APPLIED
===========================================================
gione, hae, hitorimushi-ga, keshinohana, rikka, shincha, sudare,
takamushiro, tokiwagiochiba, yoshikiri — all now show their full 4
exemplar poems. Root causes:

  - 8 terms (gione, hae, hitorimushi-ga, keshinohana, sudare,
    takamushiro, tokiwagiochiba, yoshikiri) had a wrong or
    over-decorated kanji in the Saijiki sheet's Kigo_JP column,
    so the poem-matching code (which matches on exact kanji) never
    found their poems even though the poems existed and were
    correctly tagged. Corrected Kigo_JP to match summer.md and the
    poems' own Kigo tags:
      gione:            祗園会 -> 祇園会
      hae:              蝿 -> 蠅
      hitorimushi-ga:   火取虫（蛾） -> 火取虫
      keshinohana:      けしの花 -> 罌粟の花
      sudare:           簾（青簾） -> 簾
      takamushiro:      簀 -> 簟
      tokiwagiochiba:   常磐木落葉 -> 常緑木落葉
      yoshikiri:        葭切 -> 葦切

  - rikka: its 4 poems' Kigo field led with English text ("Start of
    summer 立夏 rikka"), and the matcher only reads the leading run
    of kanji/kana, so it found nothing. Reformatted those 4 poem
    rows' Kigo field to "立夏  Rikka  [Summer / Season]" so the
    kanji leads.

  - hanabi / shincha: the Saijiki sheet had two rows apiece sharing
    the same Kigo_JP (hanabi as Summer + hanabi-2 as Autumn; shincha
    as Life + shincha-2 as Plants). The generator's poem-matching is
    a plain {kigo_jp: slug} dict, so the second row silently stole
    all the first row's poems. Per your call, kept hanabi as Summer
    and shincha as Life; deleted the hanabi-2 and shincha-2 rows and
    their now-orphaned pages, and fixed the 8 poem pages whose
    "Season word" backlink pointed at the deleted -2 pages.

  - aoba: left as-is (0 exemplar poems) by design — every poem in
    the collection near this term is tagged with the combined term
    若葉・青葉 and is thematically about young leaves (wakaba), not
    mature green leaves (aoba). You confirmed aoba and wakaba should
    stay separate rather than share wakaba's poems, so aoba has no
    exemplar poems until an aoba-specific poem is added to the
    collection.
