The way Mt. Fuji appears in the fifth lunar month. The snow begins to melt and its rough surface can be seen, a majestic display of nature in its summer form. In the rainy season, Fuji is often clouded over and can only occasionally be seen, but on clear days one can admire it, soaring above the fresh green of the surrounding area.

Variations: natsu Fuji (summer Fuji), yukige Fuji (Fuji with melting snow).
