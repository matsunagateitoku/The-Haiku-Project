Fields covered with thickly growing grasses, which give off steam in the strong sunlight. In the fourth month they are called Uzuki-no, when the green is fresh; in the fifth month they are called satsuki-no, when the rains increase the luxuriance of the grasses. There have been many poems on this subject.

Variations: ao-no (green fields), natsu-no nohara (summer fields and plains).
