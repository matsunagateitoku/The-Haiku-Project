身にしむや香炉の煙秋の風
mi ni shimu ya / karō no kemuri / aki no kaze
piercing into me— smoke from the incense burner blown by the autumn wind
Saito Tokugen
秋風の姿なりけりむらすすき

the form of the autumn wind— that is what it is: clusters of pampas grass
Kitamura Kigin

Masaoka Shiki
秋風や黒子に生えし毛一根
akikaze ya / hokuro ni haeshi / ke ikkon
The wind of autumn: a hair has begun to grow on my mole.
Akutagawa Ryūnosuke
秋風や甲羅をあます膳の蟹
akikaze ya / kōra wo amasu / zen no kani
The wind of autumn: only the carapace remains of a crab on the tray.
Akutagawa Ryūnosuke

Mizuhara Shūōshi
秋風が通るに嬰児片眼をあき
aki kaze ga / tōru ni eiji / katame wo aki
An autumnal wind passes by, and a little baby opens an eye.
Yagichi Seishi
ひとり膝を抱けば秋風また秋風
hitori hiza wo / dakeba aki kaze / mata aki kaze
All alone, I hug my knees...an autumnal wind, again, autumnal wind.
Yagichi Seishi

The autumnal wind surpasses an empty snakeskin in whiteness.

Passing by, I see an autumnal wind blowing at a pine tree’s wound.
