The kanji say "bath robe," as it was originally worn on coming out of a bath. It is unlined, and at present is used more generally on a summer evening to cool off and browse along night markets, or to enjoy summer festivals and fireworks. Blue and white were common colours, but now all colours have come to be used, with varying patterns, large and small.

Variations: ai yukata (deep blue yukata), furu yukata (old yukata).
