mosquito
蚊  ·  6 poems
わが宿は　蚊のちいさきを　馳走也
Waga yado wa / ka no chiisaki wo / chiso nari
My house — tiny mosquitoes are the treat.
Basho
蚊ばしらの　際ほのぼのと　三日の月
Kabashira no / kiwa honobono to / mika no tsuki
A swarm of mosquitoes — at their edge, dimly, a crescent moon.
Bokudo
昼の蚊を　後にかくす　仏かな
Hiru no ka wo / ushiro ni kakusu / hotoke kana
A daytime mosquito, the Buddha hides it behind him.
Issa
叩かれて　昼の蚊を吐く　木魚かな
Takakarete / hiru no ka wo haku / mokugyo kana
When struck, out came a daytime mosquito — sutra drum.
Natsume Soseki
蚊の声や夜深くのぞく掛け鏡
ka no koe ya / yo fukaku nozoku / kakekagami
A mosquito’s whine: in the depth of night I peer into a hanging mirror.
Iida Dakotsu
すばらしい乳房だ蚊がいる
subarashii / chibusa da / ka ga iru
Splendid breasts— there is a mosquito.
