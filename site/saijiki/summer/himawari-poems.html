向日葵  ·  6 poems
向日葵は　月光に堪へぬ　花をなる
Himawari wa / gekko ni taemu / hana wo naru
The sunflower — now a flower that can't bear the moonlight.
Asogi Kajiro
向日葵の　影引きずりて　日が疲る
Himawari no / kage hikizurite / hi ga tsukaru
Dragging the sunflower's shadow along, the sun, exhausted.
Sano Mamoru
向日葵に　剣の如き　レールかな
Himawari ni / tsurugi no gotoki / reru kana
By sunflowers, the rail is like a sword.
Matsumoto Takashi
向日葵の　大声で立つ　枯れても尚
Himawari no / ogoe de tatsu / karete nao
The sunflower stands with a loud voice, likewise when withered.
Akimoto Fujio
向日葵播く雲の上なる日を探す
himawari maki / kumo no ue naru / hi wo sagasu
I sow sunflower seeds and look up above the clouds to locate the sun.
Saitō Sanki
荒園の力あつまり向日葵立つ
kōen no / chikara atsumari / himawari tatsu
Concentrating the strength of the abandoned garden, a sunflower stands.
