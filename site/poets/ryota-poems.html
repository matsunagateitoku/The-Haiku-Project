Spring
馬借りてかはるがはるに霞みけり
uma karite / kawarugawaru ni / kasumikeri
Summer
緑わく夏山陰の泉かな
Midori waku / natsu yama kage no / izumi kana
Green wells up — / the spring of a summer / mountain shade.
遠里や二筋三すぢ田うゑ笠
To zato ya / futasuji misuji / taue gasa
A remote village — / two, three lines of / rice-plant hats.
夏痩のわが骨探る寝覚かな
Natsu yase no / waga hone saguru / nezame kana
The summer thin / pries his bones, / awakening.
祇園会や京は日傘の下を行く
Gion e ya / kyo wa higasa no / shita wo yuku
Gion festival — / in the capital they go / under parasols.
山寺や五色にあまる花み堂
Yamadera ya / goshiki ni amaru / hana mido
Mountain temple — / more than five colours, / the flowery pavilion.
くれなゐは　花にかぎらじ　初鰹
Kurenai wa / hana ni kagiraji / hatsugatsuo
Scarlet is / not limited to flowers — / the first bonito.
下闇に　乾かぬ　関伽の　しづくかな
Shita yami ni / kawakanu aka no / shizuku kana
In under-trees darkness / lustral water doesn't dry, / water drops.
里の灯を　ふくみて　雨の若葉かな
Sato no hi wo / fukumite ame no / wakaba kana
The village lamps / shine in them, / young leaves in the rain.
玉水の　菖蒲を走る　匂ひかな
Tama mizu no / shobu wo hashiru / nioi kana
Pure water drops / rush through the acorus — / the perfume.
