A cooling dish popular in the hot summertime as a snack. A sweet syrup is poured over a heap of shaved ice. Strawberry, lemon, boiled azuki beans, tea and sweet-rice syrup are some of the favourite flavours. The ice shop with a flag on which the kanji for kori is written provides an easy resting place, an oasis in the summer landscape. Also called natsu gori (summer ice) to distinguish it from the ice of wintertime.

Variations: kori mise (ice shop or stall).
