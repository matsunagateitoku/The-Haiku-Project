sweet-fish
鮎  ·  5 poems
鮎膾藍より青き蓼酢かな
ayu namasu / ai yori aoki / tade zu kana
sweet fish salad bluer than indigo nettle vinegar
Saito Tokugen
釣竿に鮎のあはれや水はなれ
Tsuri zao ni / ayu no aware ya / mizu banare
On the pole, the ayu pathetic — leaving the water.
Hokushi
山の色釣りあげし鮎に動くかな
Yama no iro / tsuri ageshi ayu ni / ugoku kana
The mountain’s colour on the hooked ayu moves.
Hara Sekitei
鮎釣の腰を真白に水たぎつ
Ayutsuri no / koshi wo mashiro ni / mizu tagitsu
On the ayu fisherman’s hips, pure white water foams.
Kizu Ryuga
上流は曲りて見えず鮎の川
Joryu wa / magarite miezu / ayu no kawa
Upstream, with the bend, not in sight — ayu river.
