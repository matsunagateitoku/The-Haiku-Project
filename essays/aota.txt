Ricefields that are thick with the green leaves of the rice plant. After young seedlings have been transplanted, the growth is quick; in about twenty days the green leaves are so luxuriant as to hide the water they stand in. The fields, marked in rectangles or triangles, appear like beautiful mats on the earth. When the wind blows through them, the leaves bend and sway in turn, making waves and billows.

Variations: aota nami (green-ricefield waves), aota kaze (green-ricefield wind), aota michi (green-ricefield path), aota-doki (green-ricefields time).
