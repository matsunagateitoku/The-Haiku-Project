Powder to counteract heat rash. It was dusted on children after a bath, and used by women with delicate skin. In the old days it was made of the starch of the yellow snake gourd (tenka) root, known for its cooling, moisture-absorbing properties. In modern times a mixture of zinc white and the starch of potatoes or sweet potatoes, talc, camphor and boric acid make up the composition of this powder.
