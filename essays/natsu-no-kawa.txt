In the three months of summer, rivers show various changes — from becoming full of muddy water after the rainy season, with decreasing water in mid-summer, flowing slowly and thinly, to revealing more river bed than water towards the end of the season. In mountain gorges under luxuriant growth, the water looks pure and cool, and down in the plains children may swim in the rivers.

Variations: natsu kawara (summer river bed), satsuki-kawa (fifth month river).
