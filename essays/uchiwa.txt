A thin bamboo stalk is split at the top and bent to make a frame, while the bottom half is left as a handle. Sheets of paper or silk in round, oval or square shapes are spread over and pasted on the frames on both sides. This fan is used to make a cool breeze, drive away mosquitoes and flies, and to start a charcoal fire. Whereas a folding fan is used on formal occasions when going out, the round fan is homely and informal, and goes well with a yukata on a summer evening.
