Mostly the fine silk kimonos of women, worn to go out, are written of. Very cooling to see, the forearms that show through the transparency of the silk is charming. There are many kinds of thin silk used — silk gauze, meshwork, crinkled silk, and so on, some as thin as the wings of a cicada. Also called usu goromo or usuginu.

Variations: sukiya (transparent silk), keira (light, thin silk), chijimi (crinkled silk).
