Kobayashi Issa
小林一茶  ·  23 poems
Spring
大蛇や恐れながらと穴を出る
ōhebi ya / osore nagara to / ana wo deru
a large snake― / says, “pardon me” and / exists his hole
春の日や水さへあれば暮れ残り
haru no hi ya / mizu sae areba / kure-nokori
a spring dayー / where there’s water / dusk lingers on
小酒屋の出現したり春の山
ko-zakeya no / shutsugen shitari / haru no yama
a small sake stall / has appeared on / the spring mountain
Summer
麦秋や 子を負いながら いわし売り
mugi aki ya / ko wo oi nagara / iwashi uri
autumn of wheat- / with a child on her back / sardine peddler
人声に子を引きかくす女鹿かな
Hito goe ni / ko wo hiki kakusu / mejika kana
At people’s voices / mother deer draws fawn to her / and hides it.
我が宿に一夜たのむぞ蚊喰鳥
Waga yado ni / hitoyo tanomu zo / kakuidori
To my house, / please come a night! / Bird that eats mosquitoes.
蟾どのの妻や待つらん子鳴らん
Hiki dono no / tsuma ya matsuran / ko nakuran
Mr. Toad’s / wife must be waiting, / his child, crying.
夏山や一足づつに海見ゆる
Natsu yama ya / hito ashi zutsu ni / umi miyuru
Summer mountain — / with each step / the ocean in view
松陰や寝茣蓙一つの夏座敷
Matsu kage ya / negoza hitotsu no / natsu zashiki
The shade of pine trees — / one rush mat to sleep on, / summer parlour.
大猫のどさりと寝たる団かな
O neko no / dosari to netaru / uchiwa kana
The big cat, / with a thud, lay on it, / round fan.
虫干に猫も干されて居たりけり
Mushiboshi ni / neko mo hosarete / itari keri
For insect airing / the cat, too, / being aired.
蕗の葉にいわしを配る田植かな
Fuki no ha ni / iwashi wo kubaru / taue kana
In butterbur leaves, / sardines passed around — / rice-planting.
一番に乙鳥のくぐるちのわ哉
Ichiban ni / tsubame no kuguru / chinowa kana
First of all, / a swallow went through, / lalong wreath.
新茶の香真昼の眠気転じたり
Shincha no ka / ma hiru no nemuke / tenji tari
The scent of new tea — / noon-time sleepiness / has gone away.
灌仏の御指の先や暮の月
Kanbutsu no / oyubi no saki ya / kure no tsuki
At the bathed / Buddha's august finger-tip, / the dusk moon.
祭り見に　ぶらぶら下がる　毛虫かな
Matsuri mi ni / burabura sagaru / kemushi kana
To see the festival, / it strolls down — / caterpillar.
やれうつな　蠅は手をする　足をする
Yare utsuna / hae wa te wo suru / ashi wo suru
Don't swat it! / The fly rubs its hands, / its feet.
昼の蚊を　後にかくす　仏かな
Hiru no ka wo / ushiro ni kakusu / hotoke kana
A daytime mosquito, / the Buddha hides it / behind him.
紫陽花の　末　一色と　なりにけり
Ajisai no / sue iashoku to / nari ni keri
The end of / hydrangeas — all became / one colour.
青梅に　手をかけて寝る　蛙かな
Ao ume ni / te wo kakete neru / kawazu kana
On a green plum / its hand placed, / the frog asleep.
塔ばかり　見えて東寺は　夏木立
To bakari / miete Toji wa / natsu kodachi
Only a pagoda / can be seen, the East Temple, / a summer grove.
僧となる　子のうつくしや　けしの花
So to naru / ko no utsukushi ya / keshi no hana
The child who's / to be a monk, beautiful! / Poppy flowers.
湯上りの　尻にべったり　菖蒲かな
Yu agari no / shiri ni bettari / shobu kana
On the rump / just out of the bath, / an acorus leaf.
