Matsuo Bashō
松尾芭蕉  ·  36 poems
Spring
春立つや新年ふるき小目五升
haru tasu / shinnen furuki / go-shō
spring begins― / for the new year, five pints / of old rice
春雨や蜂の巣つたふ屋根の漏
harusame ya / hachi no su tsutau / yane no mori
spring rain― / along a bee nest / the roof leak
よく見ればなづな花咲く垣根哉
yoku mireba / nazuna hanasaku / kakine kana
look closely: / the shepherd's purse is / flowering at the hedge
Summer
六月や峯に雲置あらし山
roku-gatsu ya / mine ni kumo oku / arashi yama
the sixth month- / a cloud set on its peak / Mt. Storm
araumi ya / sado ni yokotau / Amanogawa
The rough sea— / Extending toward Sado Isle, / The Milky Way.
夏の夜や崩れて明けしひやし物
natsu no yo ya / kazurete akeshi / hiyashi mono
a summer nightー / light breaks over / chilled dishes
這いでよ飼屋が下の蟾の響
Hai ideyo / kaiya ga shita no / hiki no koe
Come on out! / From under the coop / a toad’s voice.
ほととぎす大竹藪を漏る月夜
Hototogisu / o takeyabu wo / moru tsukiyo
Little cuckoo — / through a large bamboo grove, / the moonnight.
ほととぎす鳴いて行方や島一つ
Hototogisu / naite yukue ya / shima hitotsu
Little cuckoo — / in the direction it faces, / an island.
鶯や竹の子藪に老を鳴く
Uguisu ya / take no ko yabu ni / oi wo naku
The bush warbler — / in a grove of bamboo shoots, / sings of old age.
能なしの寝むたし我をぎやうぎやうし
No nashi no / nemutashi ware wo / gyogyoshi
To sleepy me, / with no abilities, it sings — / reed warbler.
水鶏啼くと人へいへばや佐屋泊り
Kuina naku to / hito no ie baya / Saya domari
“A water-rail sings,” / as someone said, / stop at Saya.
馬ぼくぼく我を絵に見る夏野かな
Uma bokuboku / ware wo e ni miru / natsu no kana
The horse, clop... clop... / I see myself in a picture, / summer fields. / A ku of 1682. As the country horse walks slowly in the hot weather, the writer imagines they must appear like a picture.
島々や千々に砕けて夏の海
Shimajima ya / chijini kudakete / natsu no umi
Island after island — / into a myriad, crumbled, / the summer ocean. / A ku about Matsushima, a series of numberless small islands, which Basho described as amazing, and a wondrous work of heaven's creation. Pine trees grow on each little island.
掬ぶよりはや歯にひびく泉かな
Musubu yori / haya ha ni hibiku / izumi kana
Just by scooping, / a sting to the teeth — / the spring.
さざれ蟹足這いのぼる清水かな
Sazare gani / ashi hai noboru / shimizu kana
A little crab / crawls up my foot — / pure water.
ひとつ脱でうしろに負いぬ更衣
Hitetsu nuide / ushiro ni oi nu / koromogae
Took off a layer, / flung it on my back — / change of garment. / During a journey, not having a summer garment, he merely took off a layer of the clothes he was wearing.
山を庭に動き入るるや夏座敷
Yama mo niwa ni / ugoki iruruya / natsu zashiki
Even the mountain / seems to move into the garden — / summer parlour.
風流の初やおくの田植うた
Furyu no / hajime ya oku no / taue uta
The source of sweet simplicity — / a north province's / rice-plant song.
皿鉢もほのかに闇の宵涼み
Sara hachi mo / honoka ni yami no / yoi suzumi
Plates and bowls / faint, feel cool on / a dark evening.
吹く風の中を魚飛ぶ御祓かな
Fuku kaze no / naka wo uo tobu / misogi kana
In the breeze / fish are jumping, / purification.
灌仏の皺手合はする数珠の音
Kanbutsu no / shiwa te awasuru / juzu no oto
Bathed the Buddha — / wrinkled hands together, / rub the rosary.
鎌倉を生きて出でけん　初鰹
Kamakura wo / ikite ideken / hatsugatsuo
They must have / left Kamakura alive, / the first bonito.
頓て死ぬ　けしきは見えず　蝉の声
Yagate shinu / keshiki wa miezu / semi no koe
Soon to die, / it does not seem — / the cicada's voice.
閑さや　岩にしみ入る　蝉の声
Shizukasa ya / iwa ni shimi iru / semi no koe
The stillness — / into boulders sink / cicada voices.
梢より　あだに落ちけり　蝉のから
Kozue yori / ada ni ochikeri / semi no kara
From tree-top / in vain it fell, / a cicada shell.
わが宿は　蚊のちいさきを　馳走也
Waga yado wa / ka no chiisaki wo / chiso nari
My house — / tiny mosquitoes / are the treat.
かたつぶり　角ふりわけよ　須磨明石
Katatsuburi / tsuno furiwake yo / Suma Akashi
Oh, snail, / wave your horns apart, / Suma, Akashi.
駿河路や　花橘も　茶の匂ひ
Suruga-ji ya / Hana tachibana mo / cha no nioi
The road through Suruga — / even the citrus flowers / have the scent of tea.
先づ頼む　椎の木もあり　夏木立
Mazu tanomu / shii no ki mo ari / natsu kodachi
First, a pasania / tree to rely on, / summer grove.
嵐山　薮の茂りや　風の筋
Arashiyama / yabu no shigeri ya / kaze no suji
Arashi mountain — / through the luxuriance of groves / a line of the wind.
須磨寺や　吹かぬ笛聞く　木下闇
Suma dera ya / fukanu fue kiku / Koshita yami
Suma temple — / an unblown flute heard, / under-trees darkness.
清滝や　波に散り込む　青松葉
Kiyotaki ya / nami ni chiri komu / ao matsuba
Kiyotaki — / into its waves scatter / green pine needles.
白芥子や　時雨の花の　咲きつらん
Shira geshi ya / shigure no hana no / saki tsuran
White poppies — / as though flowers of joy rain / have bloomed.
あやめ草　足にむすばん　草靴の緒
Ayama gusa / ashi ni musuban / waraji no o
Acorus grass, / I'll tie on my feet, / as sandal straps.
New Year
甲比丹もくばはせけり君が春
kapitan mo / tsukuba hase keri / kimi ga haru
the Capatin also, / came to bow to; / our Lord's spring
